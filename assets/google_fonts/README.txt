Lato font files for the google_fonts package.
Expected names (exactly): Lato-Light.ttf, Lato-Regular.ttf, Lato-Bold.ttf
Get them with tool/fetch_lato.sh or from https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Lato
(Download family -> static/). Lato is licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1;
keep OFL.txt next to the fonts.
